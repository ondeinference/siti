- Use, modify, and redistribute the **code** under Apache-2.0.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Ship a fork to the App Store, Google Play, or any store **using the name
  "Siti" / "Siti AI"** or the Siti icons/logos.
- Name your product, or imply it is endorsed by us, in a way that could confuse

If you fork and distribute, **rename** the app and replace the icons and brand
assets. Keeping the code open while reserving the brand is deliberate: it lets
the community learn from and build on Siti while protecting users from
impersonation.
