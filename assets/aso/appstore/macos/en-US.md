100% ON YOUR MAC
Siti's AI runs entirely on your Mac. No data is ever sent to a server — your conversations never leave your computer. After a one-time model download (~1–2 GB), Siti works completely offline. Use it on your MacBook during flights, at a cafe, or anywhere without Wi-Fi.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Unlike assistants that send your data to cloud servers, Siti runs entirely on your Mac. No account, no sign-in, no cloud processing, no tracking, no data collection. Your conversations never leave your computer.

BUILT FOR YOUR MAC
Siti is a native macOS app that takes full advantage of Apple silicon for fast, fluid AI conversations. The clean interface feels right at home on your Mac, and the on-device model leverages your hardware for quick, private responses.

WORKS ANYWHERE, ANYTIME
On a plane, on the road, off the grid — Siti is always ready. Because everything happens on your Mac, you get fast, private answers wherever you are.
