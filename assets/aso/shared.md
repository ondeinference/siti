## Brand Tagline

"Profoundly personal. Entirely private."

A playful nod to "Siri AI" — but Siti flips the script: instead of the cloud,
everything stays with you. The name is also a warm, familiar Indonesian and
Malay feminine name, giving the assistant an approachable, human identity.

## Brand Voice

Clean, confident, calm, and privacy-first — with a human touch. Minimal in the
spirit of great product design: say less, mean more. Siti is the assistant that
keeps your world to itself.

## Positioning One-Liner

Siti AI is a private AI assistant that runs 100% on your device. Ask anything,
completely offline. No cloud, no account, no tracking.
