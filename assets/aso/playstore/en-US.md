Meet Siti — your private AI assistant that runs entirely on your device 🌼

Siti is a profoundly capable, profoundly personal assistant that never sends your words to the cloud. Ask questions, draft messages, brainstorm ideas, summarize text, translate languages, or just talk things through — all powered by an on-device AI model that keeps every conversation completely private.

🤖 ASK SITI ANYTHING

• Get clear answers to everyday questions
• Draft emails, messages, and posts
• Brainstorm ideas and make plans
• Summarize long text in seconds
• Translate between languages
• Explain difficult topics simply
• Get help with writing and editing
• Learn something new, one question at a time

📱 100% ON YOUR DEVICE

Siti's AI runs entirely on your phone. No data is ever sent to a server — your conversations never leave your device. After a one-time model download (~1–2 GB), Siti works completely offline. No internet connection needed.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No data collection

Your conversations are yours alone.

✈️ WORKS ANYWHERE, ANYTIME

On a plane, on the subway, off the grid — Siti is always ready. Because everything happens on your device, you get fast, private answers wherever you are.

⚡ FAST AND NATIVE
